Pixel Weather Overlays (Free): what's new
=========================================

2.0
  - 11 new overlays: lightning flash, sunbeams, cloud shadows, wind, aurora, shooting stars, smoke, hail, acid rain,
    snow drift and dandelion seeds (38 in total, each at 16 px and 32 px)
  - lightning bolt strikes (3 shapes, both sizes) to fire over a storm
  - 7 weather recipes: ready-made stacks like thunderstorm, blizzard, misty morning and summer night, plus
    time-of-day tints (dawn, dusk, night, storm and more)
  - Godot 4 weather controller: pick a recipe, press Play; crossfades, tint and lightning included
  - shader intensity setting: 1 to 3 copies for heavier weather from the same file
  - dust, pollen, sparkles, fairy dust, embers, bubbles and sprinkles are fuller and easier to see
  - every 1.x file keeps its name and folder, so you can copy 2.0 straight over your old folder

1.0
  - first release: 27 overlays at 16 px and 32 px, Godot 4 shader and script
